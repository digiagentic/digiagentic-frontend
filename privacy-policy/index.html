Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

DigiAgentic is a full-service digital agency. When this policy says "we", "us", or "our", it means DigiAgentic and its team. Our website address is www.digiagentic.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — your name, email address, phone number, company name, and website URL — when you fill in a contact form, request a free audit, or send us an email.

We also collect standard technical data through cookies and analytics tools: your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on each page, and referring URL. This information helps us understand how people use the site and improve the experience.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, deliver the service you requested, send you relevant updates (only with your consent), and improve our website and services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to remember your preferences and understand how you use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some parts of the site may not work as expected without them.

We use Google Analytics to understand site usage. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised traffic data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party services to help us operate the website, process enquiries, or deliver our services. These providers only have access to the information necessary to perform their function and are required to protect your data in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. You can request deletion at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can also withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we do our best to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch.
